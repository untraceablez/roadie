# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Roadie to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith and give clear, kind feedback on issues and pull requests.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information (including credentials and tokens) without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate or harmful.

Report problems to the maintainer, **untraceablez** on GitHub, by opening a private [security advisory](https://github.com/untraceablez/roadie/security/advisories/new) or contacting them through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
